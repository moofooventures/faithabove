---
title: What to Do With Anxious Thoughts
date: 2026-09-21
topic: Anxiety
verse_ref: Philippians 4:6-7
verse_text: Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
description: Paul doesn't tell us to stop feeling anxious. He tells us what to do with it.
---
Paul wrote this letter from a Roman prison, which is worth sitting with for a second. This isn't a verse written by someone with an easy life telling other people to relax. It's written by a man in chains who had every earthly reason to be anxious.

He doesn't say "don't feel anxious" — feelings aren't a switch you flip. He says "be careful for nothing," meaning don't let worry be the thing you carry alone. Instead: prayer, supplication, thanksgiving. Three specific actions.

**A simple pattern to try tonight:**

- **Name it** — say exactly what you're anxious about, out loud or on paper.
- **Ask** — bring the specific request to God, not a vague "help me."
- **Thank** — name one thing you're grateful for in the middle of the situation, even if the situation itself hasn't changed.

The promise isn't that the circumstance resolves. It's that peace — a peace that doesn't make logical sense given the circumstances — stands guard over your heart and mind. That's the phrase: it "keeps" you, like a soldier keeping a post.

PRAYER: Father, I bring You what's weighing on me right now, specifically, instead of carrying it in vague dread. Thank You for what is still good even in the middle of this. Guard my heart and my mind with a peace I can't manufacture myself. In Jesus' name, amen.
